Fantasy Points

Here’s a **plain-English explanation of AFL Fantasy points**, what they mean, and **exactly how they’re calculated**.

---

## What are Fantasy points?

**Fantasy points** are a numerical score given to each player based on **how much they contributed statistically in a match**.

They’re used in:
- **AFL Fantasy (official)**    
- Casual fantasy leagues    
- Media analysis (as a quick “impact” snapshot)    

The idea is simple:  
👉 _Every action on the field is worth a set number of points._

---

## AFL Fantasy (Official) scoring system

This is the **standard AFL Fantasy** scoring (not SuperCoach — that’s different).

### Core scoring actions

|Action|Fantasy Points|
|---|---|
|Kick|**+3**|
|Handball|**+2**|
|Mark|**+3**|
|Tackle|**+4**|
|Free kick for|**+1**|
|Free kick against|**–3**|
|Hitout|**+1**|
|Goal|**+6**|
|Behind|**+1**|

---

## What _doesn’t_ count?

These **do not earn points directly**:
- Contested vs uncontested possessions    
- Inside 50s    
- Clearances    
- Intercepts    
- Disposal efficiency %    
- Pressure acts    

(They matter in real footy — just not directly in Fantasy scoring.)

---

## Example: how a player’s score is built

Let’s say a midfielder finishes with:
- 20 kicks → 20 × 3 = **60**    
- 10 handballs → 10 × 2 = **20**    
- 8 marks → 8 × 3 = **24**    
- 6 tackles → 6 × 4 = **24**    
- 1 goal → **6**    
- 1 free kick against → **–3**    

### Total Fantasy score:

**60 + 20 + 24 + 24 + 6 – 3 = 131 points**

That’s a **very strong AFL Fantasy game**.

---

## Why some players score better than others

Fantasy rewards:

- 🏃 **High possession players**    
- 🔁 **Midfielders and rebounding defenders**    
- 🧱 **Tackling machines**    

Fantasy penalises:

- Key forwards (low disposals, goal-reliant)    
- Lockdown defenders    
- Players with poor discipline (free kicks against)

## Typical Fantasy score ranges

|Score|Meaning|
|---|---|
|40–60|Poor / quiet game|
|60–80|Average|
|80–100|Solid|
|100–120|Very good|
|120+|Elite / dominant|

---

## Key Fantasy concepts explained simply

### 📈 Ceiling
- A player’s **best possible score**    
- High ceiling = match-winning Fantasy player    

### 📉 Floor
- A player’s **worst likely score**    
- High floor = consistency    

### 🔄 Role dependency
- Fantasy scores depend heavily on **role**, not talent    
- Same player, different role = different score
    

---

## Important distinction: AFL Fantasy vs SuperCoach

|AFL Fantasy|SuperCoach|
|---|---|
|Fixed points per stat|Points weighted by impact|
|Easy to calculate|Uses Champion Data formula|
|Rewards volume|Rewards efficiency & damage|
